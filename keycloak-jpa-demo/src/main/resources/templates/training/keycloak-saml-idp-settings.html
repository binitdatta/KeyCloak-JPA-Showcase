End-to-end relationship

Microsoft Entra ID authenticates a user and produces a SAML response. Keycloak receives that response at its broker endpoint, checks the trusted IdP and applicable security conditions, identifies or provisions the local user, and imports configured claims. Imported entraRoles values do not by themselves authorize a user to perform privileged operations.

Before deploying: verify Entra issuer and metadata, Keycloak ACS and entity ID alignment, signing certificates and signature verification, NameID consistency, attribute naming, first-login account linking, least-privilege authorization, and logout behavior.
