EPISODE E · CIBA · BACKCHANNEL AUTHENTICATION

CIBA — Live DemoThe app asks. The user approves on another device.

The client never sees the user's password. It sends a backchannel request naming the user, Keycloak notifies the approval site on localhost:8092, and the client polls the token endpoint until the user approves or denies. This page runs the same steps as the curl procedure.

Try it live

One click sends the backchannel request, opens the approval site, then polls until you approve or deny — against your actual local Keycloak, not a mock.
