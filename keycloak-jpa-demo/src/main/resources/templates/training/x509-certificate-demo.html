X509 CertificateThe certificate itself is the credential.

No secret, no signed token in the request body — the client proves its identity during the TLS handshake itself, by presenting a certificate Keycloak was configured to recognize in advance. This calls Keycloak's mTLS listener on :8443 for the token exchange, then uses the resulting access token exactly like any other bearer token.
